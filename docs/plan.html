Money Matters: product plan

A personal Android app that keeps complete, double-entry books for every rupee and every money decision. It tracks stocks, mutual funds and FDs, projects leeway and runway, and points out spending patterns worth changing. Covers everything agreed so far; last updated 20 Oct 2026.

Overview

The baseline is feature parity with Realbyte's Money Manager: accounts, categories, budgets, cards, recurring entries, stats, calendar, backup, passcode, multi-currency. On top of that the app adds:

True double-entry

  • Every transaction balances (debits = credits)
  • Real balance sheet, income statement, cash flow, trial balance

Accountability

  • Lock on reconcile, explicit month-end close
  • Decision tags, impact prompt, weekly regret review

Leeway & runway

  • Safe-to-spend and daily allowance until payday
  • Runway ladder: cash → +FDs → +markets (stress-tested)

Investments

  • Stocks, mutual funds, FDs with lots and FIFO
  • Weekly prices, explicit download-status flags

Indian tax

  • Income-tax Act 2025, tax year Apr–Mar
  • Rules stored as data per tax year

Insights

  • Rules engine computes on device
  • AI writes the weekly summary from aggregates only

Transaction lifecycle & month-end close

Draft  →  Posted  →  Reconciled (locked)  →  Month closed (locked, permanent)
  • Draft / Posted: editable, and deletable (an investment, deposit or SIP delete removes its entries from every page and recalculates; see invest-plan.md section 12). Every edit and every deletion goes to the audit log.
  • Reconciled: locked. Corrections are a reversal plus a new entry.
  • Month closed: no create, edit or delete for anything dated in that month, enforced by SQLite triggers. No reopen.
  • Months close in order. A later month can't be closed before an earlier one.
  • Late discoveries are posted in the current open month as prior-period adjustments linked to the closed month.
  • Tamper evidence: each closed month stores a hash of its entries chained to the previous month's hash, verified at app start.
  • Year end (31 Mar): closing entries move net income to Equity:Retained Net Worth.

Close checklist (all required)

  1. No drafts in the month
  2. Suspense / uncategorized account is empty
  3. All recurring entries for the month are posted
  4. Each account's closing balance is confirmed against the bank app, statement or wallet; any difference is resolved or posted to Expenses:Unaccounted with a note
  5. Card statements and EMIs are matched
  6. In Transit is clear (no stale SIP allotments)
  7. Holding units confirmed against the CAS / broker holdings
  8. Price snapshot confirmed: every holding is ✅ Verified or ✍️ Manual-confirmed
  9. FD accrued interest and revaluation entries posted

Leeway & runway

Leeway: how much can I spend right now?

Safe to spend   = liquid balance
                − bills, EMIs, card dues and SIPs due before next payday
                − this period's savings / sinking-fund target
                − buffer
Daily allowance = Safe to spend ÷ days until next payday

The month starts on payday (custom start day). For irregular income, the optional Assets:Income Buffer pays you a fixed monthly amount, and leeway is calculated from that steady amount.

Runway ladder: how long can I last?

Measured against essential (need-tagged) monthly spend. Each holding counts at its liquidation value, not its market value:

Liquidation value = Market value × (1 − stress drop) − exit costs − tax if sold
StepIncludesStress dropExit costs & tax
1. CashSavings, wallet0%None
2. + FDsFixed deposits0%Premature-break penalty calculated per FD (none within 30 days of maturity)
3. + MarketsEquity MFs / stocks30% / 40%Exit load, charges, capital-gains tax calculated per lot

The dashboard shows all three, e.g. "5 mo cash · 12 mo with FDs · 24 mo worst case", plus a separate trajectory runway that projects income, recurring bills and average spend forward. Irregular income counts at its 25th-percentile month.

Accountability

  • Decision tags on each expense: need / want / investment and planned / unplanned. Defaults are learned per category.
  • Impact prompt when one spend, or that day's spends combined, reaches ≥ 50% of remaining leeway. Transfers, card payments, reserved bills and sinking-fund moves don't count. When leeway is ≤ 0, every discretionary spend triggers it.
    It shows leeway before and after, the new daily allowance, the runway change in days, and the budget hit. Options: Confirm with reason · Save as draft · Cancel.
  • Budget overspend must be acknowledged with its source (another category, savings, or "overspent") and is recorded as a budget transfer.
  • Weekly review of unplanned and want spends, each marked worth it or regret. Regret rate per category.
  • Investment reasons on every buy and sell (goal, thesis, holding period), with a reminder to revisit later.
  • Missed SIPs are flagged with their effect on the goal date.

Investments: stocks, mutual funds, FDs

  • Holdings are asset accounts with a commodity (units). Postings carry the quantity, unit price and rupee amount.
  • Every purchase is a lot (date, units, cost including stamp duty and brokerage, lock-in date, 31 Jan 2018 value for grandfathering). Sales use lots FIFO.
  • Book value (cost) lives in the ledger. Market value comes from weekly prices. Net worth on Home and Accounts uses market value (cost for any holding with no price yet), labelled "as of last price". The official revaluation entry is still posted once a month at close.
  • Trade entry: one net rupee amount per trade (brokerage, STT and stamp duty are not itemised).
  • EPF is an asset account flagged "not in net worth"; it is shown in its own group and excluded from net worth, the wealth goal and runway.
  • Investing is not spending. Savings rate and investment rate are reported separately.
InstrumentTracked
StocksLots, broker/demat account, dividends (with TDS), bonus & split adjustments, short-term vs long-term per lot
Mutual fundsUnits, lots, In Transit for SIPs (NAV allotted T+1), equity/debt/hybrid type, ELSS 3-year lock per instalment, exit-load window; debt funds bought after 1 Apr 2023 always taxed at slab rate

Portfolio features

  • XIRR per holding, per goal and for the portfolio, from the ledger's own cash flows
  • Actual vs target allocation (equity / debt), drift alert at ±5%. Rebalance with new money first; selling comes with a tax estimate.
  • Concentration limit: no single stock above 10% of equity (editable); overlapping funds flagged
  • Insights: idle cash above the emergency target, FDs maturing soon, lock-ins ending

Prices

  • Mutual funds: AMFI's public daily NAV file (semicolon-separated: scheme code, ISINs, name, NAV, date). Wound-up schemes sit in the file frozen at their last NAV, so the row date is always checked.
  • Stocks: NSE's public end-of-day price file (UDiFF format). One file covers every stock, so it's one download a week. NSE has moved this file before (July 2024) and is changing distribution again in 2026, so the source URL is a setting. Downloads use browser-like headers, retries with backoff, and rate limiting.
  • FDs: no price needed. Value is calculated from the rate and compounding.
  • Matching by ISIN, never by name. A stock saved without an ISIN is matched on its exchange symbol instead (series EQ first); a fund with no ISIN can't be matched and is listed as such in the download log.
  • Schedule: Saturday morning, for the last trading day (NSE holiday calendar aware).

Price status badges

StatusMeaningAllowed at close?
✅ VerifiedDownloaded, ISIN matched, date = expected trading dayYes
✍️ ManualEntered by you, with source and dateYes, after you confirm it
⚠️ MismatchManual price differs from a later download by more than 1%No, until you pick one
🟡 StaleNewest available price is older than the expected date (frozen NAV, suspended stock)No, until you confirm or override
🔴 Download failedPlain-language reason: 404 file moved, 403 blocked, format changed, ISIN missingNo. Enter manually.
🟣 SuspiciousMoved more than 20% in a week; possibly an unrecorded split or bonus. A zero or negative price is never stored: it is skipped and reported in the download logNo, until confirmed or the corporate action is recorded
A status bar at the top of the Prices screen always shows the health of each source, e.g. "NSE: 🔴 failing since 12 Oct · last success 5 Oct · reason: file not found (404)". A failure is never hidden behind an old price.

Goals

Emergency fund: ₹5,00,000

  • Separate from the wealth goal; held in savings and FDs.
  • Checked against 9 × essential monthly spend. Warns if essential spend rises above ~₹55,500/month.
  • Suggested FD ladder (e.g. 5 × ₹1L), so an emergency only breaks what's needed.
  • Until it's funded, surplus goes here first by default (can be turned off).

₹1 Crore invested by age 30

  • Deadline: 20 Nov 2029, about 37 months from 19 Oct 2026.
  • Measured on: market value of stocks, MFs and FDs, excluding the emergency fund.
  • Starting point: minimal current exposure, treated as about ₹0 until holdings are entered.
  • Monthly investable amount: X, not yet known. The app works it out from 2–3 months of actual data (income − essentials − wants − sinking funds − emergency-fund top-up).
Assumed annual returnMonthly SIP needed from ₹0 (37 months)
7% (debt-heavy)≈ ₹2,44,000
10% (balanced)≈ ₹2,33,000
The required amount depends far more on how much you invest than on returns. Over about 3 years, changing the return assumption barely moves the number. The goal screen shows required SIP vs X, the projected value on 20 Nov 2029, the shortfall, and a "markets fall 30% that year" line. With about 3 years left, the horizon rule suggests starting balanced and gliding toward debt as the date approaches.

Tax (India)

The Income-tax Act, 2025 took effect on 1 April 2026, replacing the 1961 Act. Sections are renumbered (e.g. old 80C is now Section 123, with the ₹1.5L limit kept in substance), and "previous year / assessment year" is now a single Tax Year. Rules are keyed by concept with both old and new section labels, and stored in versioned per-tax-year tables. Every figure is verified against the official Act text at build time.
  • Regime comparison: new (default) vs old, estimated monthly. New regime: ₹12L rebate, ₹75K standard deduction for salaried people.
  • Salary: gross pay, standard deduction, TDS per payslip, EPF.
  • Freelance: gross receipts, presumptive taxation (old 44ADA, 50% deemed profit) vs actual expenses, and TDS deducted by clients as a receivable.
  • Advance tax: required once liability after TDS exceeds ₹10,000. Reminders on 15 Jun, 15 Sep, 15 Dec and 15 Mar (15 / 45 / 75 / 100%).
  • Deductions: progress against limits, flagged by whether each counts under your chosen regime.
  • Capital gains: per lot. Short- vs long-term by holding period (12 months listed equity, 24 others), ₹1.25L equity LTCG exemption tracker, gains-harvesting nudge in Feb/Mar, losses carried forward up to 8 years.
  • FD interest: taxed on accrual, with TDS credit.
  • GST alert as freelance receipts approach ₹20L.
  • Year-end pack to cross-check against Form 16, AIS and 26AS.

Estimates and records for your own planning and for sharing with a CA. The app doesn't file returns or give tax advice.

Insights & AI

Ledger → Rules engine (on device, deterministic) → Facts JSON → AI → Weekly summary
  • Rules: subscription detection and price rises, category creep vs 3-month average, budget pacing, small-spend leaks, duplicates and unusual charges, card-interest risk, runway change, regret rate, allocation drift, goal shortfall.
  • AI receives only computed aggregates, never the raw ledger or account numbers, and writes the narrative. It never does the math.
  • Payload preview: a screen shows exactly what would be sent. Opt-in; the rules-only summary works without AI.
  • Frequency: one weekly summary, plus immediate alerts only for urgent items. Dismissing a type of alert repeatedly mutes it.
